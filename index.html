Job Market Paper

Behavioral Responses to Air Quality Information: Implications for Optimal Policy

Air quality alerts change outdoor activity, but the behavioral response depends on recent alert history. I combine quasi-experimental evidence with a model of alert policy to study how these dynamics affect the welfare-maximizing alert threshold.
