Job Market Paper

Behavioral Responses to Air Quality Alerts and Optimal Alert Policy

Tyler Stoeger

This paper studies behavioral responses to air quality alerts and the implications of those responses for optimal alert policy. I combine air quality forecasts, alert issuances, and foot traffic data to estimate whether people reduce outdoor activity after alerts, whether responsiveness changes with recent alert history, and how those behavioral patterns affect the welfare-maximizing alert threshold.
