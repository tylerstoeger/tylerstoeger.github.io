ECON 300 — Econometrics

University of Illinois Chicago

Summer 2024 Spring 2025 Fall 2025

An undergraduate course in econometric methods focused on developing the tools and intuition needed to evaluate empirical economic research. The course emphasizes causal inference and applied econometrics, moving from potential outcomes and randomized experiments through regression, fixed effects, differences-in-differences, and regression discontinuity, with applied work in R.
